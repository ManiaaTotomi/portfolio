# AI in Design Workflow

## How Mania uses AI in her day-to-day work

Mania uses AI as a thinking and execution tool across different stages of the design process, not just for product features.

### Where AI is used

- Early exploration: to break down vague problems and explore directions
- Writing and clarity: refining UX copy, simplifying explanations, and testing phrasing
- Structuring work: organizing flows, identifying edge cases, and validating logic
- Feedback loop: challenging decisions, stress-testing ideas, and getting alternative perspectives

### Tools and usage

- Uses tools like ChatGPT / Claude to:
  - explore product decisions
  - simulate user thinking
  - refine UX copy and flows

- Uses AI coding tools (like Codex / Cursor) to:
  - prototype ideas faster
  - make small UI changes directly
  - better communicate with engineers

### Team-level impact

Mania does not only use AI individually—she also introduces it into the team’s workflow.

- Introduced Cursor-based prototyping to help designers move faster from idea to interactive output
- Uses AI tools to reduce dependency on engineering for early validation
- Encourages a more hands-on, iterative design approach

### Agents and process design

Mania experiments with building AI agents to support design work:

- Creates role-based agents (e.g. product designer, UX researcher)
- Uses them to:
  - challenge design decisions
  - simulate feedback
  - explore alternative approaches
- Treats AI as a system of collaborators rather than a single tool

### Important principle

AI is not used to make decisions.

- It supports thinking
- It accelerates iteration
- Final decisions always come from product understanding and judgment

### How this differs from AI Builder

- AI Builder = product feature for users
- AI in workflow = internal tool for speed and clarity

These are separate uses of AI.

## What this says about Mania

- Treats AI as part of the design process, not just a feature
- Combines product thinking with technical curiosity
- Pushes design toward more autonomous and faster workflows
- Uses AI to extend capability, not replace judgment

## Best answer angle

When asked about AI usage in daily work:
- focus on workflow, tools, and thinking
- mention Cursor and prototyping when relevant
- mention agents as an extension of design process thinking
- do not default to AI Builder unless explicitly asked
