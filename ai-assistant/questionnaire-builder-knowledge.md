# Questionnaire Builder

## What it is
The Questionnaire Builder is the core of the Pollfish platform. It’s where users design surveys, define structure, and shape the full research experience.

It evolved over several years from a simple question creator into a complex, flexible system supporting advanced research methods.

## Problem
Users needed to:
- create both simple surveys and advanced research studies
- manage increasing complexity (logic, quotas, research methods)
- maintain clarity while editing large questionnaires

The challenge was not just adding features, but keeping the experience understandable as complexity grew.

## Product insight
The core problem was not lack of capability—it was **managing complexity without overwhelming users**.

## Approach

### Evolution, not a single redesign
This was not a one-time redesign, but continuous evolution over multiple iterations.

### Early versions
- basic question creation
- limited flexibility
- simpler interactions

### Later evolution
- introduced structured question types and categories
- added advanced capabilities (logic, quotas, research methods)
- redesigned layout to support editing at scale

## Key design decisions
- Split the experience into clear modes (Questions, Preview, Logic)
- Designed a two-panel layout:
  - main canvas for questions
  - side panel for settings
- Made each question self-contained with its own configuration
- Used progressive disclosure:
  - basic actions visible
  - advanced actions hidden behind menus
- Kept preview always accessible for validation

## Core capabilities
- Wide range of question types (basic, comparative, specialized)
- Support for research methods (e.g. conjoint, MaxDiff, pricing models)
- Advanced logic for conditional flows
- Loop & merge to reduce repetition
- Inline validation and error handling

## Design challenges
- Preventing the UI from becoming overwhelming as features increased
- Balancing flexibility with usability
- Helping users understand logic without technical knowledge
- Maintaining consistency in a system that evolved over years

## Outcome
- The builder became a flexible system used for multiple research use cases
- Supported both novice and advanced users through layered complexity
- Reduced friction in creating and editing surveys over time

## Important nuance
- This is not a feature, but a system
- Most decisions were about managing complexity, not adding capabilities
- Trade-offs were constant:
  - clarity vs flexibility
  - simplicity vs power
